Instantaneous difference plots. Differences can arise from wind-farm wakes, but they may also result from small differences in the timing and development of atmospheric features between the two simulations. This is particularly relevant in convective conditions, when wind patterns can be patchy.

Methods

The 10 m field uses WRF U10 and V10. At 50, 100, 200 and 250 m AGL, destaggered wind components are interpolated independently to a constant height above local terrain. The plotted field is |UWF-existing| − |UCTRL|. Direction arrows are derived from the earth-relative CTRL wind.
